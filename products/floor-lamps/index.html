Floor Lamps

Modern floor lamps for living rooms, lounges and hotel suites. Arc, tripod, column and reading models in black, brass and brushed nickel finishes.

7 models in this series

Floor lighting fills the volume a room cannot reach from the ceiling. Our floor lamp range covers arc lamps for sofas, tripod and column models for corners and living areas, adjustable reading lamps for bedrooms, and IP65-rated versions for terraces. Metal finishes run from matte black to aged brass, paired with opal, silicone and linen diffusers. All models can be specified for OEM projects with private label, custom finish and packaging.
