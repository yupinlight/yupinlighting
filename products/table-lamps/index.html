Table Lamps

Table Lamps

Modern table lamps for bedside, console and hospitality settings. Alabaster, ceramic, crystal and cordless models with opal, linen and crystal diffusers.

8 models in this series

Table lighting carries the detail of a room. Our table lamp range covers bedside pairs, console and sideboard pieces, task lamps for desks and hotels, and rechargeable cordless models for restaurants and terraces. Bases are offered in alabaster, ceramic, travertine, crystal, brass and powder-coated steel, with opal glass, linen and crystal diffusers. All models can be specified for OEM projects with private label, custom finish and packaging.
