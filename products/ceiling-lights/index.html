Ceiling Lights

Ceiling Lights

Flush ceiling lights in square and round formats with metal frames, white acrylic, opal glass and alabaster diffusers.

16 models in this series

Ceiling lights carry general illumination in bedrooms, corridors and commercial interiors. The range includes square and round flush mounts, thin metal-frame panels and alabaster diffusers that soften the light across a room. Most models run on 3000K with 92+ CRI, and AC LED versions are available for projects that require mains-voltage drivers. Custom housing colour and diffuser material can be quoted for OEM orders.
