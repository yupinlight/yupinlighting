OEM & ODM Services

OEM & ODM Services

Private label, custom packaging, new mould development, inspection and certification support for lighting buyers working with Imperial Lighting.

Build the fixture your project needs

Imperial Lighting has produced lighting for European and North American buyers since 2013. We work as the manufacturing partner behind our customers’ own brands: you define the specification, we handle tooling, production, inspection and documentation.

What we can customise

Private label. Laser or silk-screen your logo on the product, and we produce custom rating labels and nameplates for your market. Artwork files are checked against the housing surface before production.

Packaging. Custom colour boxes, printed instruction manuals, barcode labels and export carton marks. We can supply packaging mock-ups for approval before the bulk run.

ODM and new moulds. If the existing catalogue does not match your design, our tooling partners can develop a new housing or diffuser. Mould drawings and samples are approved before mass production.

In-process and pre-shipment inspection. Each order is checked during production and again before dispatch. Inspection reports with photographs are issued for every shipment on request.

Certification support. We provide test reports and technical files for the certifications we already hold, and we cooperate with your nominated laboratory when your market requires additional testing.

Logistics. FOB, CIF and DDP terms are available. We work with your forwarder or quote freight through our own partners.

How a custom order runs

  1. Requirement and reference — you send drawings, reference photos and target quantity.
  2. Quotation and tooling decision — we confirm price, MOQ and whether a new mould is required.
  3. Sample production — pre-production samples are made for your approval.
  4. Order and deposit — production is scheduled after the deposit and approved sample.
  5. Production and inspection — in-process checks, then a pre-shipment inspection with photos.
  6. Shipment and documents — packing list, commercial invoice and test reports are issued with the shipment, FOB, CIF or DDP as agreed.

Step durations are confirmed per project. Where timing depends on tooling or material supply, the schedule is stated in the quotation rather than assumed.

Compliance

We can supply CE-related test reports and RoHS reports for the models we produce, and we state clearly which certificates we hold. Certification must match your target market, and the final compliance declaration remains with your brand as the importer. We do not represent that every certificate is held for every model.

Request a quotation and include the model, target quantity and market so we can respond with the correct specification.
