Wall and vanity lighting has to work at close range, so diffuser quality matters as much as the metal finish. This range includes compact sconces for corridors and stairways, horizontal vanity bars for bathroom mirrors, and slim linear fixtures for hotel guest rooms. Selected models offer 5CCT adjustable colour temperature, letting one SKU serve several project specifications. Finishes include black, brushed nickel, brushed gold, white and dark green.
