Pendant lighting defines the mood of a room. Our pendant range covers single-head drops for bedside and kitchen islands, multi-head clusters for dining tables, and linear pendants for long counters and hospitality spaces. Metal bodies are finished in black, chrome, brushed gold or aged brass, with clear bubble acrylic, opal glass, frosted acrylic and alabaster diffusers. All models can be specified for OEM projects with private label, custom finish and packaging.
