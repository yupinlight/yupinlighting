Exterior wall lighting is specified for weather exposure, maintenance access and consistent output. Our outdoor range uses sealed aluminium housings, frosted glass or acrylic diffusers and integrated AC LED sources. Formats include vertical rectangular lamps for facades and compact models for terraces and entrances. All models are produced in our Jiangmen factory and can be supplied with project-specific labels and packaging.
