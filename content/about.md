Imperial Lighting is a lighting manufacturer based in Jiangmen, Guangdong, China. Since 2013 we have produced decorative and architectural LED fixtures for importers, project suppliers and private-label brands, mainly in Europe and North America.

Our range is built around six families: pendant lights, wall and vanity lights, ceiling lights, table lamps, floor lamps and outdoor wall lamps. Within each family the models share a common design language — slim metal bodies, considered diffusers and a finish palette of black, chrome, brushed nickel, brushed gold, aged brass and selected colours.

We manufacture rather than trade. Aluminium die-casting, metal finishing, LED assembly and testing take place in our own facility, which lets us control dimensional accuracy, finish consistency and delivery schedules. For buyers who need a different housing, diffuser or packaging, we also work on ODM projects with new tooling.

## Engineering and structure first

Most lighting failures are mechanical long before they are electrical: a housing that distorts under heat, a joint that opens at the diffuser, a suspension point that was never calculated. That is why our engineering team works on the structure of the fixture first, and treats the light source as the last thing to fit.

**Sections, not shells.** Housings are developed from aluminium extrusion and die-cast profiles with defined wall thickness, rib positions and joint geometry. The profile drawing fixes where the LED board sits, how the diffuser locates, how the driver compartment is separated and how the unit is fixed to the ceiling, wall or track — before any tooling is cut. Linear and track formats are assembled from the same extruded family, so a 600 mm and a 1500 mm version share one cross-section and one set of end caps.

**Tolerance is measured, not assumed.** Profiles and castings are checked against drawings with calipers and gauges on arrival, and again at each assembly station: diffuser seating, fixing torque, gap consistency along the length of a linear body, alignment of multi-head pendants. A fixture that does not close cleanly on the bench is rejected there, not discovered on site.

**Heat, sealing and load.** Thermal path from LED board to housing, driver placement and clearance are part of the structural design rather than an afterthought. For outdoor models the same applies to gasket compression and sealed cable entries. Suspension points, canopy plates and wall brackets are sized for the finished weight of the unit, including the glass and diffuser.

**New structure on request.** For ODM projects we develop new extrusions, die-cast moulds and assembly jigs, build a working prototype for approval, and take the design through to production. Send a sketch, a reference fixture or a target specification and our engineers will come back with a proposed section, fixing method and tooling plan.

If you are sourcing lighting for a hotel, residential development or retail range, the fastest route is to send us the model numbers you are interested in along with your target quantity and market. We will reply with a quotation, lead time and the certification documents available for that model.
