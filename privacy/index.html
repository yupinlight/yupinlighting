Privacy Policy

How Upriem collects, uses and deletes the personal data submitted through this website.

Browse the catalogue Request a quote

Information we collect

This website has no user accounts, no shopping cart and no online payment. The only personal data we collect is what you submit through the inquiry form: your name, email address and message, plus any optional fields you complete (company name, WhatsApp number, country or target market, inquiry type, estimated quantity) and any files you attach.

We also collect aggregate visit statistics through a cookie-free analytics service: page views, referring source and landing page. This data is not linked to your identity.

Why we use it

We use inquiry data for one purpose: to reply to your request for a quotation or product information, and to continue the commercial conversation that follows. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who receives it

Form submissions are delivered to our sales mailbox through FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service. Website statistics are processed by our analytics provider. Product images and catalogue PDFs are served from Cloudflare R2. We do not share inquiry data with any other third party.

How long we keep it

Inquiry records are retained for ×× months from the last contact, after which they are deleted. Files you attach are stored with the inquiry email and deleted on the same schedule.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@upriem.com with the subject line “Data request”. We will confirm and action the request within 30 days.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking cookies. The analytics service is cookie-free. If this changes, this page will be updated before the change takes effect.
