01 — Manufacturing capability

What the factory does

Decorative lighting is a specification business. These are the six things we control in-house, and the reasons buyers keep their tooling with us.

01

Aluminium die-casting

Housings, frames and heat sinks cast on site, so finish and dimensional tolerance stay under one roof.

02

LED assembly

LED boards, drivers and diffusers assembled and wired in Jiangmen, then powered on before packing.

03

OEM & ODM

Private label, custom finishes, new diffusers and new moulds for buyers who need their own product.

04

Quality control

Incoming inspection, in-process checks, ageing tests and pre-shipment inspection with photographic reports.

05

Certification support

Test reports and technical files for the certificates we hold, plus cooperation with your nominated lab.

06

Export logistics

FOB, CIF and DDP shipments with packing lists, invoices and inspection documents prepared for your market.

04 — Working with us

From inquiry to shipment

Six steps, one accountable factory. You always know which stage your order is in, and what document comes with it.

  1. 1

    Inquiry

    Send the models, target quantity and market. Drawings or reference photos help.

  2. 2

    Quotation

    We confirm price, MOQ, lead time and the documents available for each model.

  3. 3

    Sampling

    Pre-production samples are made for approval before the bulk order is scheduled.

  4. 4

    Production

    Materials are checked in, assembled on line and checked at each station.

  5. 5

    Inspection

    Ageing and power-on tests, then a pre-shipment inspection with photographs.

  6. 6

    Shipment

    Packing list, invoice and test reports issued with the shipment, FOB, CIF or DDP.
