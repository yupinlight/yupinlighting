04 — Working with us

From inquiry to shipment

Six steps, one accountable factory. You always know which stage your order is in, and what document comes with it.

  1. 1

    Inquiry

    Send the models, target quantity and market. Drawings or reference photos help.

  2. 2

    Quotation

    We confirm price, MOQ, lead time and the documents available for each model.

  3. 3

    Sampling

    Pre-production samples are made for approval before the bulk order is scheduled.

  4. 4

    Production

    Materials are checked in, assembled on line and checked at each station.

  5. 5

    Inspection

    Ageing and power-on tests, then a pre-shipment inspection with photographs.

  6. 6

    Shipment

    Packing list, invoice and test reports issued with the shipment, FOB, CIF or DDP.
